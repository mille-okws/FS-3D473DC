# Power and Battery

## Battery

The Foston FS-3D473DC uses a rechargeable battery with a nominal voltage of approximately **3.3 V**.

> ⚠️ Always verify the voltage and polarity of the replacement battery before connecting it to the device. An incorrect battery can permanently damage the GPS or create a safety hazard.

### Battery Replacement

Replacing the battery requires opening the device and accessing the battery connection on the main board.

#### Procedure

1. **Power off the GPS completely.**
2. **Disconnect the external power supply.**
3. Open the GPS enclosure carefully using the appropriate screws and/or plastic clips.
4. Locate the battery and inspect how it is connected to the PCB.
5. Before disconnecting anything, **identify the positive (+) and negative (−) terminals**.
6. Use a multimeter to verify the battery voltage and polarity.
7. If the battery uses a connector, disconnect it carefully.
8. If it is soldered directly to the PCB, desolder the battery wires one at a time while avoiding short circuits.
9. Install a replacement battery with the **same polarity and a compatible voltage**.
10. Secure the battery so that it cannot move or become damaged when the device is reassembled.
11. Reconnect the battery and verify that there are no signs of excessive heating, smoke, or unusual behavior.
12. Reassemble the enclosure.
13. Connect external power and verify that the device starts and charges normally.

## Safety Notes

The battery chemistry and charging characteristics should be identified before replacing it.

Do **not** assume that any 3.3 V rechargeable battery is compatible. The replacement must be compatible with the device's charging circuit and protection system.

Never short-circuit the battery terminals, puncture the cell, expose it to excessive heat, or connect it with reversed polarity.

If the original battery is swollen, damaged, leaking, or excessively hot, stop the procedure and replace it safely rather than continuing to use it.
